# Role Onboarding Checklist

This document provides a structured checklist to help team members understand their role and responsibilities within the OctoAcme project management framework.

---

## Overview

When a new team member joins or transitions into a role, use this checklist to ensure they understand:
- Their core responsibilities
- How they interact with other roles
- Key processes and workflows they participate in
- Relevant documentation and resources

---

## Onboarding Steps for All Roles

### Understanding Role Basics
- [ ] Read the role summary in `octoacme-roles-and-personas.md`
- [ ] Review your role's responsibilities and goals
- [ ] Understand your role's typical communication channels and meeting cadences
- [ ] Review interactions with other roles to understand dependencies

### Understanding Project Processes
- [ ] Review `octoacme-project-management-overview.md` for high-level framework
- [ ] Review the process documents relevant to your role:
  - **All roles**: `octoacme-project-initiation.md`, `octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md`
  - **Project/Change planning**: `octoacme-project-planning.md`, `octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md`, `octoacme-risks-and-communication.md`
  - **Quality/Release focused**: `octoacme-release-and-deployment.md`
- [ ] Understand how your role contributes to each phase of project delivery

### Practical Setup
- [ ] Get access to project management tools (project boards, issue tracking, documentation wiki)
- [ ] Join relevant Slack channels or communication channels
- [ ] Schedule introductory meetings with key collaborators
- [ ] Understand escalation paths and approval workflows for your role

---

## Role-Specific Onboarding

### Developers
- [ ] Review the definition of "done" for code contributions
- [ ] Understand the code review and testing expectations
- [ ] Get access to repositories and development environments
- [ ] Connect with Product Managers and Project Managers to understand current priorities
- [ ] Understand how to identify and escalate technical risks

### Product Managers
- [ ] Review the current product vision and roadmap
- [ ] Understand the customer base and key use cases
- [ ] Get access to analytics, user research, and market data
- [ ] Schedule introductions with Product Managers, key stakeholders, and engineering leadership
- [ ] Review success metrics and key outcomes for current initiatives

### Project Managers
- [ ] Review active projects and their current status
- [ ] Understand the project planning and estimation process
- [ ] Review the risk register template and escalation procedures
- [ ] Get access to project planning tools and templates
- [ ] Schedule introductions with team leads and key stakeholders

### Quality Assurance Leads
- [ ] Review quality standards and acceptance criteria for current projects
- [ ] Understand the testing strategy and test automation framework
- [ ] Review defect tracking and severity classification processes
- [ ] Get access to testing tools and environments
- [ ] Connect with Developers to understand current test coverage and quality gaps
- [ ] Understand release readiness criteria and sign-off procedures

### Change Managers
- [ ] Review the change management process and change request templates
- [ ] Understand impact assessment frameworks
- [ ] Review recent changes and change history
- [ ] Get access to change tracking tools and approvals workflows
- [ ] Connect with Project Managers to understand current project changes
- [ ] Understand escalation and approval authorities

### Business Analysts
- [ ] Review the current backlog and active user stories
- [ ] Understand the requirements elicitation process
- [ ] Get access to requirements documentation and specification templates
- [ ] Connect with Product Managers and key stakeholders to understand business context
- [ ] Review acceptance criteria frameworks and validation processes
- [ ] Understand how requirements flow into development and testing

---

## Ongoing Learning

- [ ] Schedule monthly check-ins with your manager or role sponsor
- [ ] Participate in retrospectives to learn from projects and identify process improvements
- [ ] Contribute to documentation updates based on lessons learned
- [ ] Share knowledge with new team members joining your role
- [ ] Stay informed about process changes and updates to this documentation
