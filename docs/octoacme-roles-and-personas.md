# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads establish and maintain quality standards for project deliverables. They oversee testing strategies, coordinate quality activities across the team, and ensure that releases meet or exceed quality thresholds before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and acceptance criteria for deliverables
- Plan and coordinate testing activities (unit, integration, user acceptance, performance, security)
- Work with Developers to identify and prioritize defects
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and quality documentation
- Provide sign-off or escalation decisions on release readiness
- Track and report quality metrics and trends to Project Managers and Product Managers
- Collaborate with team members to investigate root causes of defects

### Goals
- Ensure project deliverables meet quality standards and customer expectations
- Reduce defects reaching production environments
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and documentation
- Support continuous improvement in development and testing processes

### Typical Communication
- Quality status reports and metrics dashboards
- Defect tracking and prioritization meetings
- Release readiness assessments and sign-offs
- Collaboration with Developers on test results and issue resolution

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide quality feedback, work together on defect resolution, and coordinate test automation efforts
- **Product Managers**: Validate that features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status, escalate release blockers, and support project planning
- **Change Managers**: Coordinate quality impact assessments for significant changes

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers facilitate and oversee significant changes to project scope, schedule, technology, or processes. They assess change impacts, communicate implications to stakeholders, and ensure smooth adoption and implementation of changes.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate change requests and assess impacts on scope, schedule, resources, budget, and risk
- Lead impact analysis discussions with cross-functional teams
- Develop and communicate change proposals to stakeholders
- Coordinate approvals and decisions for major changes
- Manage change-related communications and stakeholder updates
- Support project teams in implementing approved changes
- Track change status and metrics
- Facilitate discussions on change trade-offs and alternatives

### Goals
- Ensure changes are evaluated thoroughly before approval
- Minimize disruption and unplanned rework due to scope creep or scope changes
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and buy-in on significant project decisions
- Document change rationale and decisions for future reference

### Typical Communication
- Change request documentation and impact assessments
- Stakeholder briefings and decision memos
- Change logs and status updates
- Approval tracking and audit trails

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide impact assessments to support project planning and timeline adjustments
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on scope and priority trade-offs
- **Developers**: Assess technical feasibility and effort implications
- **Quality Assurance Leads**: Evaluate quality and testing impact of changes

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical implementation. They gather, clarify, and document requirements; create detailed user stories and acceptance criteria; and ensure alignment between stakeholder expectations and project deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Clarify ambiguities and edge cases in requirements
- Create detailed user stories with acceptance criteria
- Develop use cases, process flows, and requirement specifications
- Act as a liaison between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Validate that proposed solutions meet business objectives
- Support Product Managers with market research and competitive analysis (when needed)
- Participate in requirements reviews and refinement sessions

### Goals
- Ensure clear, actionable requirements reach the development team
- Reduce rework and misalignment due to unclear or missed requirements
- Support faster decision-making by providing complete business context
- Improve customer satisfaction through requirement clarity and validation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story specifications
- Requirement review and refinement sessions
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification notes
- Acceptance criteria validation and sign-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on backlog refinement, prioritization, and user research
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements, answer questions, and support design discussions
- **Project Managers**: Provide requirement clarity to support planning and estimation
- **Quality Assurance Leads**: Ensure acceptance criteria are testable and comprehensive
